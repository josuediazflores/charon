# Where to put the microphone for your next call

I’d put the microphone on the **desk stand** for your next call. In your [stand trial](desk-stand.md), typing interrupted one of six clips. In the [keyboard-side trial](keyboard-side.md), it interrupted four of six. Both trials used the same desk and keyboard.

That gives you a place to start tomorrow. Twelve short clips can’t tell us how the stand will sound when you’re talking and typing for several minutes at a stretch. A longer call would be a good follow-up.

## What I compared

I compared the number of clips where the trial notes said typing interrupted the voice. Each clip counts once, even if several keystrokes came through. That gives both positions the same six chances to pick up an interruption.

## How I reached the recommendation

1. **Opened the two trial notes** in your Voice project and checked how many clips each contained.
2. **Compared the interruptions.** The stand had one interrupted clip and five clear clips. The keyboard-side position had four interrupted clips and two clear clips.
3. **Saved the finding with both sources.** For the next test, use the stand on a longer call and jot down any moment when typing makes your voice hard to follow.
